## Become a 2026 Sponsor! 🐾

We're looking for sponsors for **K9 Campout 2026**! Sponsorship helps us keep the event affordable and accessible while providing valuable exposure for businesses that serve our community.

Whether you're a vendor, maker, retailer, or community organization — we'd love to have your support. Sponsorship opportunities include:

- **Logo and branding** on our website and event materials
- **On-site presence** at the event (vendor table, demos, giveaways)
- **Social media shout-outs** to our community
- **Exclusive discount codes** for attendees

Interested? Reach out to us on [Telegram](/telegram) or contact [Undefined](https://undefined.charity/contact) for more information about sponsorship packages.
