We've been busy behind the scenes wrangling sites, tags, and buses — and things are finally taking shape! 🐾 Here's everything you need to check before **August 14–16** at Camp TRC.

## 🗓 The Draft Schedule Is Up!

Feast your eyes on the [**2026 draft schedule**](/schedule) — from Sloppy Sliders and the Welcome Meet & Sniff on Friday, to Pup Games, Puppy Swim, and the Bad Dog Party on Saturday, to Brunch 'n' Beg on Sunday. 🍔🏊🐕

It's still a **draft**, so times and locations may shift with the weather and site conditions. But now's a great time to start planning your weekend.

## 🍽 A Note on Meals

You'll notice the schedule lists **meals** throughout the weekend. Meat **can** be served on the side / not included in the main dishes, so there are options if you don't eat it. But if you have **other dietary needs or restrictions**, please **plan your own camping meals accordingly** — we can't guarantee every dish will fit every diet. 🥗🍖


## ✅ Check Your Details — Please!

Head over to the [**Ticketed Puppies list**](/2026/ticketed-puppies) and check your entry carefully. We need you to confirm three things:

- **🏷 Tag Name** — is it spelled right? Engraving takes time and we want your tag to be *perfect*.
- **🚌 Bus Interest** — planning to ride with the pack? Make sure your Bus column says "Yes."
- **⛺ Site Assignment** — does it show your full site *and* spot letter (e.g. `Site 7-7E`)?

**⚠️ If your Site Assignment shows "TBD" — or has a site number but no letter (like just `Site 2`) — we don't have you fully placed yet.** Please fill out the [**update form**](/k9campout26-update) so we can get you sorted and camping happy. 🐶

## 🎟 Still Want to Come? There's Room!
