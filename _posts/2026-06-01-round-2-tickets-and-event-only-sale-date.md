Big ticket update incoming! 🎉

## 📅 Key Dates

- **Monday, June 8 at 5:00 PM** — Remaining **Camping with the Pack** sites go on sale.
- **Monday, June 15** — **Event Only** tickets go on sale.

## 🏕 Missed Out on a Pack Site? You're Still Welcome!

**TRC will never turn away a camper.** While Camping with the Pack gets you a guaranteed, named site alongside your friends, you can still book camping directly with TRC by grabbing an **Event Only** ticket — and join in on all the fun!

**Heads up on site placement:**

- **Sites 1–8** are reserved exclusively for Camping with the Pack.
- If you don't have a site booked in that range, you'll need to find an open spot at **sites 9, 10, or in overflow** — or wherever a member of staff directs you on arrival.

## 🎫 Who Needs an Event Only Ticket?

An **Event Only** ticket is required if you're attending camp but **not** booked into a Camping with the Pack site. That includes:

- 🚐 Folks bringing an **RV**
- ⛺ Anyone staying in **overflow** or who has **booked camping directly with TRC**
- 🏡 **Seasonal members** and their **guests** staying in the **upper campground**

If that's you — make sure to grab an Event Only ticket once they drop on **June 15** so you're all set for the weekend!
