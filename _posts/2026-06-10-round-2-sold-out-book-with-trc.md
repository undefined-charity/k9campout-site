All **Camping with the Pack** sites are now sold out — thank you, pups and handlers! 🎉

## 🐾 Want to camp with us anyway? You still can — TRC will never turn away a camper.

**How to book directly with TRC:**

1. Make sure you have a TRC membership (limited is free, regular requires the annual fee) — sign up or renew at [camptrc.org](https://camptrc.org/){:target="_blank"}.
2. Book your camping directly with TRC at the [TRC event booking page](https://trianglerecreationcamp.wildapricot.org/event-6578427){:target="_blank"} (sites 1–8 are reserved for Camping with the Pack; everyone else will be at sites 9, 10, or in overflow).
3. Then grab an **Event Only** ticket from us when they drop 👇

## 🌙 Staying an Extra Night or Two?

Book those additional nights directly with TRC using the [same link above](https://trianglerecreationcamp.wildapricot.org/event-6578427){:target="_blank"}.

## 🎫 Event Only Tickets Go On Sale Monday, June 15

Required for anyone attending camp who isn't in a Camping with the Pack site — RVs, overflow, folks who booked directly with TRC, and seasonal members and their guests in the upper campground.

## 📣 TRC Regular Members — Want to Join a Tent?

If you're a **TRC Regular member** hoping to be added onto an existing Camping with the Pack tent, **do it now** — through **June 14**, your TRC member number works as a **$40 discount code** at checkout. After June 14, [reach out to Pup Roar on Telegram](https://t.me/puproar){:target="_blank"} and we'll sort you out with a discount code or refund.
